This wiki is primarily focussed on the ToSeven 500-watt DM02 motor but also has information about the 250 & 350-watt versions, and the 750 & 1000-watt DM01. There is also now a 250W/25kmh 'heavy duty' EPAC version of the DM01. More information for all motors is available on the manufacturer's website [https://to7motor.com/ here].
Disclaimer: The information listed here is a collaboration of owners’ experiences gathered from the [https://highvoltagekits.com/discord High Voltage Light Electric Vehicles Discord channel] It is not necessarily verified, and is not approved by the manufacturer. Use at own risk.

== Errors and contributions ==
If you spot any errors or wish to contribute more information, please post in the DM01 or DM02 groups in the [https://highvoltagekits.com/discord High Voltage Light Electric Vehicles Discord channel] and tag @ozcableguy.
