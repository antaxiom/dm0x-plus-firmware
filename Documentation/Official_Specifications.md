# ToSeven Mid-Drive Motor & Display Specifications

## 1. DM01 Mid-Drive Motor (1000W / 750W)
- **Source:** https://to7motor.com/motor-product/1000w
- **Rated Power:** 1000W (also available in 750W and 250W HD EPAC variants)
- **Rated Voltage:** 48V / 52V (60V/72V controller variants exist)
- **Reduction Gear Ratio:** 40.27 : 1
- **Max Cadence / Speed:** 160 RPM
- **Max Torque:** 160 N·m
- **Sensor Style:** Integrated Cadence & Torque Sensor (strain gauge / Wheatstone bridge, ~0.6–0.7V idle to 3.3V max)
- **Chainring Compatibility:** Standard 46T (Optional 38T–52T, 110 BCD 5-bolt spider)
- **Bottom Bracket (BB) Axle Options:**
  - 68mm–73mm (Chainline: 50/59mm or 52.5/61.5mm)
  - 100mm (Chainline: 66/75mm)
  - 110mm (Chainline: 71/80mm)
  - 120mm (Chainline: 76/85mm)
- **Noise Level:** < 50 dB
- **Operating Temperature:** -20°C to 50°C (Thermal rollback threshold default: 100°C)
- **Motor Weight:** 6.0 kg
- **Ingress Protection:** IP65
- **Controller Mounting:** External housing mounted to motor body
- **Wiring Loom:** 1T4 E-Bus harness (Display, Throttle, 2x Brake Cutoffs), plus Speed Sensor, Shift Sensor, and 6V Lights (Headlight + Tail/Brake light)

---

## 2. DM02 Mid-Drive Motor (500W / 350W / 250W)
- **Source:** https://to7motor.com/motor-product/500w
- **Rated Power:** 500W (also available in 350W and 250W variants)
- **Rated Voltage:** 36V – 48V (supports 52V when configured)
- **Reduction Gear Ratio:** 53 : 1
- **Max Supported Cadence:** 100 RPM
- **Peak Output Torque:** 90 N·m
- **Sensor Style:** Integrated Cadence & Torque Sensor (~0.6–0.7V idle to 3.3V max)
- **Chainring Compatibility:** Standard 42T (110 BCD 5-bolt spider)
- **Bottom Bracket (BB) Axle Options:**
  - 68mm–73mm
  - 100mm
  - 110mm
- **Motor Weight:** 3.7 – 3.9 kg
- **Ingress Protection:** IP65
- **Controller Mounting:** Internal (housed inside the motor casing)
- **Wiring Loom:** 1T3 / 1T4 E-Bus harness (Display, Throttle, Brake Cutoffs), Speed Sensor, 6V Lights (no dedicated shift sensor connector on standard DM02)
